Signs Your Drywall Needs More Than Paint

A quick patch can hide damage. The better repair starts with understanding why the crack, stain or soft spot appeared.

Water Stains or Soft Areas

Discoloration, bubbling paint or drywall that feels soft can point to moisture. The leak or condensation source should be addressed before the wall is closed and refinished.

Cracks That Return

A small stable crack may be cosmetic. A crack that repeatedly returns, grows or runs from a door or window corner deserves a closer look before another coat of compound.

Loose Tape and Nail Pops

Peeling joint tape, visible seams and recurring fastener pops can make a room look unfinished. Proper preparation matters if you want the repair to blend into the surrounding wall.

Holes and Previous Repairs

Patches that sit proud of the wall, show through paint or flex when pressed may need to be reopened and rebuilt instead of skimmed again.

Photograph the full wall.

A close-up shows the damage, but a wider photo helps show where it sits relative to windows, doors, ceilings and possible moisture sources.
