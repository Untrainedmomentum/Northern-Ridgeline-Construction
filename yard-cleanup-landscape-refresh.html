Outdoor Property Guide

Plan a Yard Cleanup That Lasts

The order matters: clear the problem areas, fix the edges and grade, then add the finishing material.

Remove Debris First

Leaves, branches, dead growth and abandoned material hide the actual condition of the yard. Cleanup gives you a clear view of drainage, bare spots and bed boundaries.

Define What Stays

Choose which beds, shrubs and borders are worth keeping. Removing material without a plan can create more maintenance later.

Correct Edges and Low Spots

Clean edges make a large visual difference. Low areas, washouts and drainage trouble should be evaluated before adding mulch or decorative rock.

Add the Finish Last

Mulch, rock and new plantings should be the final layer. That keeps fresh material from being mixed into repair work or moved twice.
