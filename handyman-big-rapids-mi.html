What counts as handyman work?

Handyman work usually means smaller repair, maintenance and improvement projects that do not require a specialized licensed trade. The exact scope can depend on the project, so Northern Ridgeline reviews the details before accepting the work.

What should I send with a quote request?

Include the property location, clear photos, a short description of what is wrong or what you want changed, and any approximate measurements you already have. If you have several jobs, send the whole list.

Why hire one person for a punch list?

Small property problems often sit unfinished because each one is too small to justify a major contractor call. Grouping several maintenance items into one visit can make it easier to finally get the list handled.
