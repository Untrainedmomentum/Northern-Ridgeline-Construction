Spring Home Maintenance Checklist

Winter can expose drainage, pavement, wall and landscape problems. A spring walkaround helps you catch them while they are still manageable.

Start With Water

Look for standing water, washed-out soil, low spots and areas where downspouts empty too close to the foundation. Check sprinkler lines and outdoor water connections once freezing weather has passed.

Walk the Hard Surfaces

Inspect asphalt, pavers, walks and approaches for heaving, settlement or new cracks. Movement often becomes easier to see after freeze-and-thaw cycles.

Check Fences and Exterior Features

Look for loose posts, leaning sections, damaged gates and fasteners that worked loose over winter. Small corrections can prevent a larger section from failing.

Inspect Interior Walls

Pay attention to water stains, soft drywall, new cracks or areas where paint is bubbling. The visible finish may not be the source of the problem, so moisture should be understood before patching.

Clean Up the Yard

Remove winter debris, redefine bed edges and identify bare or overgrown areas before adding mulch or rock. Planning the order of work avoids covering problems that still need correction.

See something that needs work?

Take wide photos and close-ups, note when you first noticed the issue, and include the property location when requesting a quote.
